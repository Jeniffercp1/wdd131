Project website plan

Site Name

JPS Language

I chose this name because it represents my family's initials and it is the name of the company my mother created to teach English to Hispanic people. The goal of this brand is to help students learn English and adapt better to the country where they live.

Optional domain availability: jpslanguage.org

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to help Hispanic people learn English in a visual, simple, and flexible way. The site will offer courses and resources for A1 and A2 levels, an initial evaluation to identify each student's level, educational images, links to books or study materials, and small grammar games. It will also allow users to study independently, but if they need more support, they will be able to request classes with a tutor through Zoom during available hours. This will make learning easier for people who have limited time and need to study according to their own schedule.

Scenarios

  • How can I know if my English level is A1 or A2?
  • Where can I find images, books, and activities to study basic English?
  • How can I contact a tutor if I need help at a specific time?

Lora will be used for main headings because it has a clear and academic style.

Poppins will be used for body text, menus, buttons, and activities because it is modern, readable, and easy to read on screens.

Reason for the Topic

I chose this topic because I am Latina, and I have seen that in my country there are still many people, especially adults, who do not know basic English. Sometimes they feel embarrassed to study or to let others know that they are learning from the beginning. For this reason, I want to create a website that helps them learn in a simple, visual, and private way. Today, thanks to technology, everyone has more opportunities to educate themselves and improve their knowledge at their own pace.
