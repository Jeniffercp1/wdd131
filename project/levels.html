Courses and resources

A1 and A2 English learning path

Each level includes simple goals, visual study ideas, and resources that help students practice without feeling overwhelmed.

A1

Starter English

A1 students learn greetings, numbers, classroom language, family words, daily objects, simple verbs, and basic sentence patterns.

  • Introduce yourself and spell your name.
  • Ask and answer simple personal questions.
  • Use present tense sentences with common verbs.
A2

Everyday English

A2 students expand vocabulary and begin speaking about routines, shopping, directions, past experiences, plans, and simple opinions.

  • Describe your daily routine and schedule.
  • Ask for help in stores, schools, or offices.
  • Write short messages with clear details.
